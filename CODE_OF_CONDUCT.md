# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the VDL Parkings project to be a welcoming, respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss technical disagreements constructively.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus on the issue, implementation or proposal rather than the person.
- Respect differing levels of experience and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers may edit, remove or decline contributions and may close discussions that do not meet these expectations.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through their GitHub profile. Include relevant context and links where possible.
